Reading Test

Last weekend, Anna went to the countryside with her family. They left home early on Saturday morning and traveled by bus. When they arrived, they visited a small farm and saw many animals, including cows, chickens, and horses. In the afternoon, Anna helped her mother prepare lunch. After that, the family walked around the village and took many photos. On Sunday, they went fishing near a quiet river. Anna caught two small fish and felt very happy. Before going home, the family bought some fresh fruit from a local market. Anna really enjoyed the trip and wants to visit the countryside again.

  1. Where did Anna go last weekend?
  2. Who did Anna go with?
  3. How did they travel?
  4. What animals did Anna see?
  5. What did Anna do in the afternoon?
  6. Where did the family go fishing?
  7. How many fish did Anna catch?
  8. What did the family buy before going home?
  9. How did Anna feel about the trip?
  10. Does Anna want to visit the countryside again?
